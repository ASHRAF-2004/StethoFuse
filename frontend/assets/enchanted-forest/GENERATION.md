# Enchanted forest artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool for the StethoFuse forest visual
direction. Original PNG sources are retained here; optimized page assets are in
`public/assets/enchanted-forest/`.

## Forest hero

`forest-hero-source.png` was generated as a wide, cinematic, premium nature
illustration. The left side stays dark and uncluttered for white copy. A large old
oak trunk occupies the right edge, with a broad mossy branch extending into the
right-center at the lower quarter as a perch for the existing approved owl. The
setting uses layered firs, fern, mist, moonlight and restrained fireflies, in deep
emerald, pine and teal. No subjects, words, logos or interface elements were
included. The final is encoded as `forest-hero-v1.webp` (1672 × 941, 217,006 B).

## Glowing deer

`glowing-deer-source.png` was generated as a full-body, transparent-background
illustration of a calm, anatomically credible adult stag in a three-quarter pose.
Its natural dark coat, luminous mint antlers, subtle leaf-like bioluminescence and
small nearby motes are designed to sit over the forest research section without
covering its text. The final is encoded as `glowing-deer-v1.webp` (1024 × 1536,
alpha preserved, 356,774 B).

## Owl perch

`public/assets/owl-poster-perched-v1.webp` is a new, versioned derivative of the
existing approved landing-perch cell from `public/assets/owl/arrival-v1/atlas.webp`.
That same pose remains the last frame of the existing arrival sequence. It is
used as the immediate and reduced-motion poster, so the owl is already perched
while the optional flight animation plays and settles. No original owl art or
animation source was modified.
