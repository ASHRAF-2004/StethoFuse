# HLS-CMDS listening sample

Yasaman Torabi, Shahram Shirani and James P. Reilly (2025).
HLS-CMDS: Heart and Lung Sounds Dataset Recorded from a Clinical Manikin using Digital Stethoscope, version 3.
https://doi.org/10.17632/8972jxbpmp.3
Descriptor: https://doi.org/10.1109/IEEEDATA.2025.3566012
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Original recording: M0111.wav, explicitly qualified non-test/development material.
The public original is its unchanged PCM samples from seconds3–11, with a new WAV
header for the eight-second excerpt. Heart/lung are corresponding excerpts of
actual outputs of StethoFuse's frozen v2 model, not the source-reference targets.
See provenance.json for exact source/model/output hashes and transformations.
All three tracks use one shared playback gain of3; files are not independently
normalized. This is a product demonstration, not independent evaluation or a
clinical claim. No T9 material is used.
