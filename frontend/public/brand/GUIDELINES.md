# StethoFuse identity

Two broad channels share an S-shaped negative space: one recording, two sources.
Use the symbol in compact navigation and favicons; use the name with it when space
allows. Do not add a tagline to small marks. Keep at least 24 units of clear space
on the 256-unit master. Minimum symbol size: 16 CSS pixels; 24 recommended in UI.
The mint favicon tile supplies contrast in both dark and light browser chrome.

Masters contain filled paths, including the outlined Chillax wordmark. No raster,
mask, filter, external font or tracing is used. Main ink #232b28; reverse #ffffff;
accent #a1ffcb. Keep both source channels one colour. Do not stretch or rotate.
The living snowy owl remains a mascot, with its existing approved renderer.
Logo exploration and visual testing do not establish trademark clearance.
