# Clinical evaluation: condition checks and chest-sound gate

Generated by `python -m research.clinical.report` from protocol `stethofuse-clinical-targets-v5` (`research/configs/clinical_targets_v5.json`). Do not edit numbers by hand.

## What was tested

Each condition model was trained and tested on **different patients** (split by patient, never by recording). Thresholds and calibration were chosen on training patients only; the held-out patients were used once. Every model is compared against a baseline that guesses the class rate; targets pooled across hospitals use a baseline that knows only the hospital, so a model gets no credit for recognising the site. Pass bars were declared before results and are not changed after seeing them.

A condition is switched on only if all hold on held-out patients: at least 10 patients with and without the condition; AUROC 95% lower bound ≥ 0.7; clearly better than the baseline; sensitivity ≥ 0.7; specificity ≥ 0.6; calibration error ≤ 0.15.

## Limits

- These are research datasets, not this app's users or devices. Passing here is not clinical validation.
- BMD-HS (valve diseases) is CC BY-NC-ND: non-commercial use only, and its test set is small.
- PhysioNet 2016 publishes no patient identity, so it is used only as a second-dataset check.
- ICBHI was downloaded from the official server with an expired TLS certificate, at the owner's choice; its counts (920 recordings, 126 patients) match the publication.
- KAUH (Jordan) and RespiratoryDatabase@TR (Turkey) are CC BY 4.0; both used Littmann 3200 stethoscopes. RespiratoryDatabase@TR's no-COPD group (COPD0) has normal spirometry but chronic symptoms; it is not a healthy group.
- The Fortis (New Delhi) lung set was downloaded but excluded: no patient identifiers or ages, duplicated files, and heart sounds inside its normal class.
- BRACETS (Aveiro and Thessaloniki) and the heart-regurgitation set (Littmann 3200, echo-confirmed) are CC BY 4.0. BRACETS Aveiro has no healthy subjects, so it is dropped for COPD.
- ZCHSound (children, three Chinese hospitals, one stethoscope model, echo-confirmed) has one recording per child. Its noisy newborn set is used only by the chest-sound gate; patent foramen ovale is in neither class.
- HeartWave, Zhou et al. 2024 and the long-term-care pneumonia study were not used: recordings not obtained or available only on request.
- No healthcare professional has reviewed these outputs.
